# Piano Nuggets — Programme and Pricing

## 1. Initial programme

The first release offers **Piano & Keyboard Lessons only**.

The programme is intended for children, teenagers and adults, from beginners to learners with previous experience. Do not publish other course offerings as available at launch.

## 2. Learning areas

The piano and keyboard programme incorporates four connected areas:

1. **Practical Piano & Keyboard** — developing the ability to play and apply musical ideas on piano or keyboard.
2. **Music Theory** — understanding the concepts and written language that support music-making.
3. **Harmony** — learning how notes and chords work together.
4. **Piano Technique** — developing coordination, control and sound playing habits.

Do not invent a formal syllabus, grade structure, exam pathway, certification or guaranteed learning timeline.

## 3. Approved fees

All prices are in South African Rand (ZAR).

### Registration

- **R250 once-off registration fee.**

### Individual tuition

- **R350 per 60-minute lesson.**

Individual monthly packages are calculated on a four-week basis:

| Frequency | Lessons per four-week month | Fee |
|---|---:|---:|
| Once per week | 4 | R1,400 per month |
| Twice per week | 8 | R2,800 per month |

The maximum offered frequency is twice weekly. Do not advertise three lessons per week.

### Studio group lessons

- **R1,500 per 60-minute group session.**
- Group size: **4–6 students**.
- R1,500 is the fee for the whole group/session, not a per-student fee.

Do not imply that this group rate is a monthly package. No group monthly package has been confirmed.

### Online group lessons

- **R350 per student per 60-minute lesson.**

This is a per-student rate, not the total fee for the group.

### Optional instructional material pack

- **R850 once-off.**
- Optional; students decide whether to purchase it.
- It contains learning material/resources covering theory, harmony, technique and the programme content.

Do not make purchase of this pack sound compulsory.

### Home lessons

Home lessons are available through instructor deployment to the learner's residence. Additional travel/deployment charges apply depending on location. The exact charge is not fixed in this document.

Display wording such as: **"Home lessons are available on request. Additional travel charges depend on your location — enquire for a quotation."**

Do not invent a travel fee or include one in a package total.

## 4. Online lesson requirements

Online students must have:
- A reliable working internet connection.
- A piano or keyboard. A full-size piano or electronic keyboard is recommended, although any usable keyboard may be used.
- Suitable sound quality so the instructor can hear the notes and musical detail clearly.

Zoom is preferred, but another platform chosen by the student may be used if it supports suitable audio quality and a smooth lesson.

A camera and microphone may be recommended as practical setup guidance, but do not present unconfirmed device specifications as strict requirements.

## 5. Pricing presentation rules

The website must clearly distinguish:
- Individual tuition: R350 per student per 60-minute lesson.
- Studio group: R1,500 for the whole group per 60-minute session, 4–6 students.
- Online group: R350 per student per 60-minute lesson.

Always show the lesson duration and pricing unit. Do not show a group total as a per-student fee or vice versa.

Registration and optional materials must be displayed separately from tuition. Home travel charges must be described as location-dependent.

## 6. Future programme possibilities

Music Production, Studio Sound Engineering, and Music Theory & Composition have been discussed as possible future courses. They are not part of the initial public offering and must not appear as currently enrolable programmes unless the owner approves a later expansion.
