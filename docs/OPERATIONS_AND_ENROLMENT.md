# Piano Nuggets — Operations and Enrolment

## 1. Lesson formats

Learners may enquire about individual or group lessons.

### Studio lessons

Students may attend lessons at the Fresh Wind Music studio. The exact public address and approved location wording must be confirmed before publication.

### Home lessons

An instructor may be deployed to teach at the learner's residence. Availability and additional travel/deployment charges depend on the learner's location.

The website should request a suburb or general area at the initial enquiry stage. Collect a full residential address only when it is genuinely required for arranging a home lesson, and explain why it is needed.

### Online lessons

Zoom is preferred, but the learner may use another mutually agreed platform if sound quality is good enough for the instructor to hear the instrument clearly.

Online learners need a reliable internet connection and a piano or keyboard. A full-size piano or electronic keyboard is recommended, but any usable keyboard may be used.

## 2. Group arrangements

- Studio group sessions accommodate 4–6 students.
- Studio group fee: R1,500 for the entire group per 60-minute session.
- Online group fee: R350 per student per 60-minute lesson.

Do not assume that the same group, students or schedule will be available for every enquiry. Group placement is arranged after contact with the prospective learner.

## 3. Enrolment journey

1. Prospective learner or parent/guardian visits the website.
2. They review the programme, lesson formats and pricing.
3. They submit the online enrolment enquiry or contact the team through the verified WhatsApp channel.
4. The team reviews the information and contacts the enquirer.
5. The team discusses suitability, location, format, group placement and schedule.
6. Fees, any home-lesson travel charge and payment arrangements are clarified.
7. The team confirms the next steps directly with the enquirer.

**A form submission is not a confirmed booking, guaranteed placement or proof of payment.**

## 4. Online form fields

Recommended initial fields:

### Learner
- Student full name.
- Date of birth or age.
- Previous musical experience (beginner / some experience / details).
- Instrument available (piano / keyboard / other details).

### Parent or guardian
- Parent/guardian full name and contact details when the learner is a minor.
- For adult learners, the learner is the primary contact.

### Contact
- Contact number.
- Email address.

### Lesson preferences
- Preferred delivery: studio / home / online.
- Preferred lesson type: individual / group.
- Preferred frequency: once weekly / twice weekly.
- Preferred days or time windows (optional, not a booking guarantee).
- For home lessons: suburb or general area.
- For online lessons: preferred platform (optional).
- Optional material pack interest: interested / not interested / would like more information.

The form should adapt sensibly to answers. For example, ask for a suburb when home lessons are selected and parent/guardian information when the student is a minor.

Avoid collecting unnecessary sensitive information. Do not request identity-document numbers, medical information or unrelated personal details.

## 5. Minors and privacy

- Make it clear that a parent or legal guardian should submit or oversee enrolment for a minor.
- Collect parent/guardian contact details for minors.
- Provide a privacy notice near the form explaining what information is collected and why.
- Do not publish student names, contact details, photographs or submitted form information.
- Obtain appropriate, explicit consent before using any learner image, video, testimonial or identifiable story in marketing.
- Define retention and deletion practices before production launch.
- Ensure the selected form/database/email setup is access-controlled.

The final privacy notice and data-handling process should be reviewed for applicable South African privacy requirements, including POPIA, before launch.

## 6. Submission and notifications

After submission:
- Validate required fields.
- Store and/or deliver the enquiry securely.
- Notify the designated business recipient through the approved channel.
- Show a clear acknowledgement that the enquiry was received and the team will make contact.
- Provide a fallback contact method if submission fails.

The destination email address, notification method and authorised people who can access enquiries are **TBC**. Do not invent them.
